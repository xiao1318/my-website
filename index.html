Asik-Asik Falls (Alamada, Cotabato)

Asik-Asik Falls was one of the most unique waterfalls I’ve ever seen! Instead of falling from a river above, the water comes straight out of the cliff wall like a curtain, and it looks unreal in person.

Donna

Traveler

Gumasa Beach (Glan, Sarangani)

Gumasa Beach was such a relaxing escape! The sand is powdery white and the water is clear and calm, which makes it perfect for swimming and just lounging by the shore.

Charisse

Visitor

Lake Sebu (South Cotabato)

Lake Sebu was one of the most memorable stops on my trip! The lake is calm and beautiful, with mountains all around, and the lotus flowers made the view even more special.

Divine

Influencer

General Santos Fish Port Complex, General Santos City

Visiting the General Santos Fish Port Complex was an eye-opening experience! Seeing the huge yellowfin tuna lined up on the trading floor, with buyers inspecting each one, showed me why GenSan is called the Tuna Capital.

Coleen

Tourist

Tuna Festival, General Santos City

Joining the Tuna Festival in General Santos in September was such a fun and lively experience! The street parades were colorful, and the ocean floats were creative, and they showed how proud the city is of being the Tuna Capital.
